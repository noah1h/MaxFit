Overview

Thank you for purchasing! This documentation will guide you through the installation and customization process.

Installation

Simply extract the ZIP and open index.html in your browser. Deploy on your server or Netlify/Hostinger etc.

Customization

Change text in index.html, update colors in :root of css/style.css, and customize interactions in script.js.
